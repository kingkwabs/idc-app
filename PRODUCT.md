# Product

## Register

product

## Users

IDC Energy operations staff, administrators, and finance users work in this application throughout the business day. They need to move transactions from client RFQ through sourcing, purchase orders, shipment, and invoicing without losing operational context or exposing internal supplier economics.

## Product Purpose

IDC Energy Operations Hub is the operational system of record for customer, supplier, transaction, shipment, document, and invoice workflows. It should make the next action obvious, preserve an accountable history, and let authorized staff complete high-risk actions such as account administration and QuickBooks invoicing with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Precise, decisive, and composed. The interface should feel like a premium business instrument: direct enough for daily operational work, confident enough for financial workflows, and recognizably IDC Energy without becoming decorative.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic glassmorphism admin template, a playful consumer app, or a finance cliche built from navy and gold. Avoid decorative background motion, excessive cards, saturated inactive states, novelty controls, and styling that competes with transaction data.

## Design Principles

1. Operational clarity first. Every screen should foreground the current state, the next action, and the information needed to make that action safely.
2. Earn trust through explicit state. Loading, errors, permissions, destructive actions, and external-system outcomes must never be ambiguous.
3. IDC Energy is the accent, not the wallpaper. Use the logo's charcoal, warm white, red, and amber relationships with restraint and purpose.
4. Familiar patterns move faster. Use consistent tables, forms, navigation, and feedback so experienced users can work without relearning controls.
5. Build for change. Shared tokens, typed API boundaries, role-aware capabilities, and isolated domain services should make new modules easier to add later.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All workflows must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic labels, color-independent status communication, reduced motion, readable contrast, and responsive use from laptop through mobile widths.
